For teams that have already built AI calls into a product or internal workflow, we offer enterprise plans separate from the personal plans, covering both the **Anthropic (Claude)** and **OpenAI (GPT)** platforms across two tiers — **Enterprise Standard / Enterprise Flagship** — for a total of **6 SKUs**.

## The problems we solve

If your team has run into any of the following, an enterprise plan is usually a better fit than a personal plan:

- **Volatility affecting production**: occasional call failures that force you to write your own fallback, retry, and monitoring in code
- **Inflexible quota structure**: day / week passes can't sustain a team's concurrent, multi-person, multi-scenario needs
- **Slow support response**: waiting hours for support when something breaks, with the business stalled
- **Missing compliance materials**: finance needs invoices, legal needs a contract
- **No visibility**: not knowing how many tokens each project spent, who's running what, or how to allocate costs

## Capabilities shared by all enterprise tiers

Whichever tier you choose, every enterprise plan includes:

## FAQ

<Callout type="info" title="Can enterprise and personal plans coexist?">

Yes. One account can hold multiple plans at once, and calls are routed by the API key's group — see [API Protocols · Protocol and available models are two different things](/en/docs/guide/api-protocols#protocol-vs-models).

</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Can I mix Claude and GPT?">

Which models one API key can call is decided by its group: if you ordered an Anthropic-platform SKU, you can call Claude-series models; if you ordered an OpenAI-platform SKU, you can call GPT-series models. When you need both platforms, order the corresponding SKUs separately.

</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="How do I negotiate a higher SLA or compensation terms?">

Above the enterprise tiers' default 99.5% availability target, if you need a higher SLA or explicit compensation terms, arrange them through the business contact; the contract governs.

</Callout>
