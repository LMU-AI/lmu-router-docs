Invite friends to sign up for LMU AI. Every time they top up or subscribe, you keep earning commission — a lifetime bind and long-term income.

---

## Rules

- When a new user signs up through your invite link, they are **bound to you for life**
- Every time they top up or subscribe, you earn a **10% book commission**
- Commission is released gradually as the invitee actually spends (top-ups track balance consumption progress; subscriptions track days elapsed)
- Available commission can be **transferred directly to your account balance** for API spending, or withdrawn to WeChat / Alipay / bank card
- New users get an extra **¥2.00** bonus on their first payment

---

## How to take part

Log in to the console and click "**Referral rewards**" in the left menu to see your dedicated invite link and invite code.

### Option 1: Share your invite link

Copy your dedicated link and send it to a friend; once they click it and sign up, the bind is complete. The format is:

```
https://api.lmuai.com/register?ref=YOUR_INVITE_CODE
```

### Option 2: Share your invite code

Tell your friend your invite code, and they enter it manually when signing up to complete the bind.

## Viewing and withdrawing

After logging in to the console, click "**Referral rewards**" in the left menu to view:

- Cumulative commission, available commission, and released amount
- Number of people invited and a breakdown of each commission
- One-click transfer of available commission to your balance, or a withdrawal request
